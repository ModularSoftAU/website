Greetings everyone,

Welcome to **Anchorlight Development** — a collective of dedicated developers building software that grounds ideas and illuminates solutions. Our team specialises in a broad range of software, from Minecraft plugins and datapacks to web-scale platforms and community tools.

At Anchorlight, we're committed to building with purpose. Every product we ship is designed to meet a real need — whether that's enriching a gaming experience, fostering spiritual reflection, or empowering community administrators.

Our current portfolio includes:

* **[Craftify](../docs/products/craftify/)** — A Minecraft datapack that elevates gameplay with revamped mechanics, 30+ new crafting recipes, and deeper progression systems.
* **[DevoteMe](../docs/products/devoteMe/)** — A faith platform designed to support Bible reading, daily devotions, and spiritual reflection, with Discord integration to meet people where they already gather.
* **[PlayerHeadHunt](../docs/products/playerHeadHunt/)** — A versatile Minecraft plugin for scavenger hunts, Easter egg quests, and custom player challenges.
* **[Zander](../docs/products/zander/)** — A full-featured Discord and server administration suite for managing communities with precision.

These are just the beginning. We have more projects in active development and we're always looking for ways to create meaningful impact across the gaming and digital landscape.

Stay tuned for updates, follow along on [GitHub](https://github.com/ModularSoftAU), and join our [Discord community](https://discord.gg/YRpakuYhJZ) — we'd love to have you along for the journey.

*— The Anchorlight Development Team*
